# Official SFM proceedings examples

The examples were extracted locally from the official Saratov Fall Meeting
2024 proceedings for layout study. The extracted PDFs are intentionally kept in
`local_artifacts/sfm_examples/` and are not committed to the repository.

## Official sources

- Proceedings index: <https://sfmconference.org/proceedings/>
- Saratov University archive: <https://www.sgu.ru/struktura/uprid/nauchnye-izdaniya-sgu/sborniki-i-prodolzhayuschiesya-izdaniya/problemy-opticheskoy>
- SFM 2024 proceedings PDF: <https://www.sgu.ru/sites/default/files/page/files/sfm-24-2-1.pdf>

## Locally extracted examples

- `sfm2024_breast_cancer_pattern_recognition_pp12-19.pdf`: an ML-oriented
  medical image classification article.
- `sfm2024_microalgae_optical_monitoring_pp7-12.pdf`: a concise experimental
  biophotonics article.
- `sfm2024_smartphone_skin_imaging_pp184-192.pdf`: an imaging article with
  multiple figures and quantitative plots.

## Formatting observations

The 2024 proceedings use a single-column A4 layout, a centered uppercase title,
author and affiliation blocks, a compact abstract/lead paragraph, numbered
sections, numbered figures and tables, conclusions, and numbered references.
The current draft follows this visual grammar while retaining stronger
statistical reporting: paired seeds, confidence intervals, explicit controls,
and a statement that the locked test remains closed.

These examples are precedents, not a verified mandatory SFM 2026 template. The
final manuscript must be checked against any template supplied by the
organizers.
